Everything a scanner should do

Features other scanners lock behind a subscription, SnapDoc gives you free — and runs them on your device, not on someone else's server.

Smart scanning

Crop & enhance

Drag the four corners to perfect the crop, rotate pages, and boost contrast and sharpness automatically — every scan comes out clean and readable.

OCR in many languages

Extract text from scans on-device with ML Kit — Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari scripts. Edit the text, search inside it, or translate it offline.

Professional PDF tools

Merge, split, extract and reorder pages, compress with quality profiles, add page numbers and watermarks, annotate, and protect PDFs with real AES-256 passwords.

Word, Excel & PowerPoint

Export any scan as genuine .docx, .xlsx or .pptx files built from its OCR text — plus PDF, JPG and plain text sharing.

Ask your library questions, total up receipts, and get instant summaries with key points — all computed on your phone.
